❄️ Webhook Keep-Warm Guide (Render Free Tier)
Render's free tier automatically puts your server to sleep after 15 minutes of inactivity. When a customer attempts to pay, Safaricom's webhook hits a sleeping server, causing a timeout and a failed payment.

To keep your server warm:
  1. Register for a free account at Cron-Job.org or UptimeRobot.
  2. Create a new monitor/cron job.
  3. Set the execution interval to every 10 minutes.
  4. Point the request URL to your server's health endpoint: https://your-server.railway.app/health
